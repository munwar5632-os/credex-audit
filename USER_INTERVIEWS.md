# User Interviews

## Interview 1 – Narayan, Service Professional

**When:** 2026-05-26

**Direct quotes:**

- "I only use free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, and Cursor because I can't afford paid plans."
- "I don't track spending – it's zero for me."
- "If a tool showed me how to get more value from free tiers, I'd use it."

**Most surprising thing they said:**
He didn't know that different free tiers have different limits – he just uses whatever works.

**What this changed about my design:**
I added a note that even free‑tier users can benefit from understanding limits, and the audit respects zero spend.

## Interview 2 – Self ( Munwar SHaik), Student / Developer

**When:** 2026-05-26

**Direct quotes:**

- "I personally pay for ChatGPT Plus at 1 doller for free oneyear and use Cursor free."
- "I've never compared pricing between Cursor and Copilot."
- "A shareable report would be useful to show my team."

**Most surprising thing they said:**
I didn't realise how much small subscriptions add up until I calculated my annual spend.

**What this changed about my design:**
I made the annual savings number prominent – it has more impact than monthly.

## Interview 3 – Mahesh, Power BI Intern

**When:** 2026-05-26

**Direct quotes:**

- "My office pays for subscriptions, but I personally only use free versions of AI tools."
- "I don't track spending because the company handles it."
- "If I saw a report showing potential savings, I might recommend it to my manager."

**Most surprising thing they said:**
Even though his company pays, he never checks if they are on the right plan – it's just approved by management.

**What this changed about my design:**
I added a "share with manager" feature (shareable URL) so interns and junior devs can easily send the report upward.
