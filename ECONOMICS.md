## Conversion Rates Needed for Profitability

Tool runs at ~$0/month (Supabase free, Vercel free, Resend free, Groq free).
Break-even: 1 credit purchase per month covers all costs.
For meaningful revenue: 20+ purchases/month = ~3,000 audits/month = ~12,000 visitors/month.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months

**Month 1-3:** 100 audits/day → 45 purchases/month → $13,500 MRR

**Month 4-9:** SEO from share page URLs. Each shared audit = Google-indexed page. 500 audits/day → $67,500 MRR

**Month 10-18:** Enterprise referrals. CTOs share tool internally. 200 enterprise customers at $500 average = $100,000 MRR = $1.2M ARR

**What must be true:**

- Share feature creates organic SEO
- At least 1 viral HN/Reddit post in first 30 days
- Credex sales team closes 30% of consultation requests
- Average credit purchase stays above $300
